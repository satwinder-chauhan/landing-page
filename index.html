The People Who Shaped Naruto

Minato Namikaze

Minato Namikaze, known as the Yellow Flash of the Leaf, was the Fourth Hokage of Konohagakure. He was famous for his incredible speed, intelligence, and selflessness.

Jiraiya

Jiraiya, also known as the Toad Sage, was one of the Legendary Sannin. He was a powerful ninja, a wise mentor to Naruto, and the author of popular novels.

Iruka Umino

Iruka Umino was a caring instructor at the Ninja Academy. He was one of the first people to believe in Naruto and treated him with kindness, helping him feel accepted.

Kakashi Hatake

Kakashi Hatake, known as the Copy Ninja, was the leader of Team 7. He was famous for his Sharingan, calm nature, exceptional skills, and dedication to his students.
